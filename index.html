PTS Pali texts from GRETIL, proofread

The Pali Text Society editions as GRETIL gives them (digital input by the Dhammakaya Foundation), with proofreading corrections against the scans of the printed editions marked in the text. Built reproducibly from a pinned commit of the GRETIL mirror; source, method and licence: github.com/jorgecaa/pts-gretil-ng.

red underline: GRETIL encloses the words in braces { }; reproduced as in GRETIL, unchanged except for notation.   green underline: GRETIL departs from the printed edition; the printed reading is given.   orange dotted underline: an emendation of the edition, suggested but not applied. Hover over (or tap) a marked word for the readings, the evidence or authority, and the approval date when recorded.

The critical apparatus (footnotes) has NOT been proofread, except in the Sutta Nipāta. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, © Pali Text Society and Dhammakaya Foundation, 2015.

Every volume listed with a file was reviewed in the foureditions review service. The counts give the corrections approved so far. A volume with 0 corrections applied has no approved correction yet; this does not mean it was proofread in full and found free of errors, since the proofreading is not finished for every book. Ja I carries only suggested emendations, which do not change the text.

Struck through, at the end: the books the review service leaves out, which are not built. A review needs a scan of the same printed edition that GRETIL transcribes; six of these books have none usable, and the Yamaka is excluded:

  • Vimānavatthu and Petavatthu, Buddhavaṃsa and Cariyāpiṭaka: GRETIL gives the later PTS editions by N. A. Jayawickrama (1977 and 1974); the scans at hand are of the earlier editions (Gooneratne 1886, Minayeff 1888, Morris 1882), a different text.
  • Apadāna: the scan at hand was withdrawn, because its images lose the diacritics.
  • Paṭṭhāna: no scan of the PTS edition is at hand.
  • Yamaka: its PTS edition is excluded from the review because of its many abbreviations.
